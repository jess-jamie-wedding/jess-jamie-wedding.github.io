Local Guide

Getting There

Direct trains available from London within 1.5 hours (St Pancras, Victoria, or London Bridge). Canterbury West train station is a 5-minute walk from St Dunstan's Church. Alternatively, there are direct trains to Whitstable, Faversham, and Canterbury East.

Where to Stay

If you are staying for one night, we recommend staying in Canterbury. If you are staying in the area for longer, we recommend staying in Whitstable.

Airbnbs: We would recommend booking an "Airbnb" to support local people and businesses. Please book direct where possible, for example Whitstable Holiday Homes, Keepers Cottages, or The Whitstable Cottage Company. Airbnb.com can be a good search tool to find others.

Hotels: Local hotels include The Pig, Crescent Turner, The Marine (Whitstable), The Falstaff (Canterbury) and The Abode (Canterbury). There is also a Travelodge and Holiday Inn Express within 10 mins.

Pubs with rooms:The Duke of Cumberland, The Millers Arms.

Camping and Glamping: Brook Farm, Rosewood and Chapter Farm.

Unusual accomodation: Whitstable Fisherman's Huts.

Things to Do

Whitstable is a seaside town with a lovely high street, shingle beaches and an active harbour. Canterbury is a very historical city with a lot of beautiful architecture.

Our favourite places to eat in Whistable include Samphire, The Lobster Shack, Whitstable Oyster Company and Tudor Tea Rooms (for ham, egg and chips). Other great local gastropubs include The Red Lion and The Sportsman

If you can, we recommend not driving to the wedding. Transport will be provided from the Ceremony to the Reception.

If you do need a car, there is no guest parking available at the Ceremony but there are other car parks nearby. Additionally, if you are in need of parking at the Reception venue, please let us know due to limited spaces.
